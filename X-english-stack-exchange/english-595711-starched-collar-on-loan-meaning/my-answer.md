# My answer — 2022-09-26

Posted by me ([Driftr95](https://english.stackexchange.com/users/463861)) on English Language & Usage Stack Exchange, <https://english.stackexchange.com/a/595797>, 2022-09-26; **accepted**; score 1. Text as retrieved 2026-10-12.

I would consider this more of a metaphor than an idiom - a starched collar is restrictive and  uncomfortable though it might make you *look* more presentable, and being "borrowed" means it can be taken away easily.
