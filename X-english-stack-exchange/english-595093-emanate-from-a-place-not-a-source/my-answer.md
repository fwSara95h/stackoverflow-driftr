# My answer — 2022-09-15

Posted by me ([Driftr95](https://english.stackexchange.com/users/463861)) on English Language & Usage Stack Exchange, <https://english.stackexchange.com/a/595137>, 2022-09-15; **accepted**; score 3. Text as retrieved 2026-10-12.

Yes, [Merriam-Webster][1] lists the example sentence *Good smells emanated from the **kitchen**.*
Also, one of their recently scraped examples included, *The glow from this particular milky sea spanned 38,000 square miles and appeared to the crew members to emanate from about **32 feet below the surface**, reports The Guardian.*, which uses a relative location like in your question. 


  [1]: https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/emanate
