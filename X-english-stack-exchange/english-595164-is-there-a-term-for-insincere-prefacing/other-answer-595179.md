# Answer by [FumbleFingers](https://english.stackexchange.com/users/2637) — 2022-09-15

Posted on English Language & Usage Stack Exchange, <https://english.stackexchange.com/a/595179>, 2022-09-15. Licensed [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/); reproduced in full as context for my answer — the text is the author's. Retrieved 2026-10-12.

It's entirely a matter of opinion how much we should classify initial *I hope everyone is having a great Monday* as somehow "incompatible" with following text lambasting the employees for not cleaning up after the Friday office party.

But in terms of "grammatical terminology", if two text "incompatible" text elements appear in the same utterance, that utterance would be an...

> [**oxymoron**][1] (Merriam-Webster)  
 *a combination of **contradictory or incongruous** words*


  [1]: https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/oxymoron
